01 / The city

MORE THAN
YOU EXPECT.

Wałbrzych combines industrial heritage, historic architecture, mountains and one of Poland's most impressive castles.

Wałbrzych is a city in Lower Silesia, surrounded by the landscapes of the Wałbrzych Mountains.


Its identity was strongly shaped by the mining industry, but today the city is also known for tourism, culture, historic architecture and nature.


From the monumental Książ Castle to the former mining complex of Stara Kopalnia, Wałbrzych tells its story through places you can actually explore.
